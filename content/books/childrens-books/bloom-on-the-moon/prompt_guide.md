Important Note on Text in AI Images:
Current AI image generators (Midjourney, DALL-E 3, etc.) are getting better at text, but they often misspell words or add gibberish.
Best Practice: Generate the image without text first, then add the text in Canva, Photoshop, or InDesign. This gives you perfect typography and control.
If you want AI to attempt the text: Use the prompts below. If the text is garbled, simply regenerate that specific page. DALL-E 3 is generally the best at getting the actual words right.
